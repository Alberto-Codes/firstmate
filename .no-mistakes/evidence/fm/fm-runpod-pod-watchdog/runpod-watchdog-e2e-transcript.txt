RunPod pod watchdog - end-to-end operator transcript
====================================================
bin/fm-runpod-watchdog.sh, branch fm/fm-runpod-pod-watchdog

WHAT THIS SHOWS
  The gap the captain authorized closing: a rented GPU kept billing after the
  agent session that rented it was replaced, because pod deletion only ever ran
  from inside an agent session. These scenes drive the real executable end to
  end and show a pod being stopped with NO agent alive, plus the states where it
  must refuse to stop anything.

HOW THE ACCOUNT IS FAKED
  RunPod's GraphQL endpoint is replaced at the process boundary by a PATH `curl`
  shim, the same way tests/fm-runpod-watchdog.test.sh does it. Every byte below
  comes from the real script; no real RunPod account was contacted and nothing
  was rented or destroyed. Timings in scenes 2-4 are compressed (poll seconds,
  ceiling in cents) so a six-hour bound can be demonstrated in seconds.
